# Support

Foyer Home Defender is the personal, non-commercial project of one person,
published as Foyer Labs. It is looked after on a best-effort basis:

- **There is no promise of an answer, a fix or a date.** Issues are read when
  there is time. Some are answered quickly, some late, some not at all.
- **An issue may be closed without discussion**, including one that describes
  a real problem the project will not work on.
- **A feature request is an idea, not an order.** Most will not be built.
- **Only the latest release**, on the Home Assistant versions it declares
  (2026.6 or later), is looked at. A fix goes into the next release, never
  backwards.
- **An issue waiting on its author** is labelled `needs info` and closes by
  itself after fourteen days without an answer. Nothing else closes by itself.
- **A donation is a thank-you.** It buys neither support nor priority.

> Foyer is offered as software that automates actions on rules, not as an
> alarm system. It is not certified (EN 50131, CEI 79-3), not monitored, not a
> fire alarm, and comes with no warranty and no promise of support
> (Apache-2.0, sections 7 and 8). Do not rely on it alone to protect people or
> property: keep certified smoke alarms, and a professional installation where
> a policy or a risk calls for one.

## Before you open an issue

1. Read [troubleshooting](docs/troubleshooting.md): most questions are
   answered there, starting with a zone that never triggers.
2. Look at the **log page**: every refusal has a row with its reason.
3. Download Home Assistant's **diagnostics** for Foyer. It is anonymised and is
   usually what turns an issue into an answer.

Then open an [issue](https://github.com/foyer-labs/Foyer-Home-Defender/issues/new/choose),
in English or Italian.

A way to disarm without a code, to keep the alarm quiet when it should sound,
or to read what should not be readable is **not** an issue: report it
privately, as [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) explains.
